# Competitor Product Research: Online Ordering, Restaurant POS & WhatsApp Ecommerce

**Research Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Products Analyzed:** Avocado POS, OlaClick, Take.app, Cococart, Atlas Kitchen, Tablevibe

---

## Executive Summary

This document provides a comparative analysis of six products spanning online ordering, restaurant point-of-sale (POS), and WhatsApp-based ecommerce. The landscape reveals distinct positioning: **Avocado POS** and **Atlas Kitchen** focus on full POS capabilities; **OlaClick**, **Take.app**, and **Cococart** emphasize WhatsApp/digital ordering; **Tablevibe** centers on direct-to-consumer ordering with zero commission. Geographic focus varies from US-only (Avocado) to LATAM (OlaClick), Southeast Asia (Atlas, Cococart, Tablevibe), and global (Take.app).

## Individual Product Summaries

### 1. Avocado POS
**Website:** https://www.avocadopos.com

**Overview:** A modern, AI-powered point-of-sale system built specifically for food trucks, coffee shops, and restaurants. Differentiates through zero monthly fees and zero merchant fees—revenue is transaction-based, aligning incentives with merchant growth.

**Key Features:**
- AI assistant for order taking, reporting, team management, loyalty, and marketing
- Demand prediction and automated inventory tracking
- QR code ordering and smart SMS notifications when orders are ready
- Mobile-first design with multi-connectivity (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, cellular)
- Flexible hardware: USB, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Ethernet printer support

**Target Customer:** Food trucks, coffee shops, small restaurants, and independent food service operators

**Markets Served:** United States (strong presence in Texas, California, Oklahoma)

**Pricing Model:** No monthly fees; no merchant/transaction fees. Transaction-based revenue model.

**Notable:** Strong focus on food truck compliance and regulatory content (e.g., Texas food truck laws, California ADDE Menu Act).

### 2. OlaClick
**Website:** https://olaclick.com/en

**Overview:** A comprehensive digital menu and ordering platform for restaurants, with deep WhatsApp integration. Trusted by 40,000+ restaurants, primarily in Latin America. Offers a free tier and commission-free digital menus.

**Key Features:**
- Digital menu with photos, customizable prices, QR code table ordering
- WhatsApp integration for orders and CRM
- Full POS with cash register and table settings
- Kitchen display system (KDS)
- Delivery management with zones, driver app, real-time tracking
- Inventory management, chatbot, loyalty programs, marketing tools

**Target Customer:** Restaurants of all sizes seeking digital transformation; strong fit for markets where ~80% of orders still come by phone

**Markets Served:** 20+ countries in Latin America; largest in Brazil (~50%) and Mexico (~25%)

**Pricing Model:** Free plan with basic features; Advanced and Premium plans from ~$8/month for POS + digital menu

**Notable:** Raised $2M seed (2021); restaurants report ~35% higher average order value and ~20% higher reorder rates.

### 3. Take.app
**Website:** https://www.take.app/features

**Overview:** WhatsApp-native ecommerce platform for restaurants and other SMBs. Enables order reception, catalog management, and automated notifications entirely through WhatsApp. Strong in Indonesia and expanding globally.

**Key Features:**
- WhatsApp order reception and automated notifications
- Order management dashboard with payment/delivery status
- Delivery and pickup time slot scheduling
- Product catalog with sizes, add-ons, real-time availability
- Cutoff time management (e.g., orders due 1 day before, 6pm cutoff)
- Meal subscriptions, analytics, loyalty features
- Order filtering and bulk actions

**Target Customer:** Solo entrepreneurs, home-based food businesses, cafes, bakeries, catering, small restaurants; also ecommerce and services

**Markets Served:** 180+ countries; strong in Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, South Africa, India, Mexico; 100,000+ registrations, 1M+ orders processed

**Pricing Model:**
| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Notes |
|------|---------|--------|-------|
| Basic | Free | Free | Unlimited WhatsApp orders, no commissions, manual payments |
| Premium | $19 | $190 | Card payments (Stripe, PayPal), analytics, loyalty |
| Business | $50 | $500 | Multi-staff, multi-store, wholesale, priority support |

**Notable:** Supports 70+ payment methods including QRIS (Indonesia), GCash (Philippines), Pix (Brazil); 10+ languages.

### 4. Cococart
**Website:** https://www.cococart.co

**Overview:** Singapore-based ecommerce platform targeting the ~200M businesses that still use WhatsApp, phone, or spreadsheets for orders. Offers online store, POS, QR ordering, and bookings in a modular, no-code setup.

**Key Features:**
- WhatsApp storefront: customers browse products, send order to WhatsApp
- Unlimited products, inventory tracking, variations, custom labels
- Multiple payment options: cash, cards, bank transfer, Venmo, PayNow, GCash
- POS system, QR code ordering, KDS, CRM
- Loyalty programs, email marketing, bookings/reservations
- 24/7 operation without owner online

**Target Customer:** SMEs, home-based sellers, local shops, F&B outlets, solo entrepreneurs in emerging markets

**Markets Served:** 90+ countries; strong in Singapore, Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam; 25,000+ merchants; expanding in India, Indonesia, Australia

**Pricing Model:**
- 14-day free trial
- Online Store: from $19/month (Pro $59/month)
- POS: Free basic; Pro $59/month
- Add-ons: Email Marketing $19/mo, Bookings $29/mo, QR Ordering/Loyalty/KDS $39/mo each
- Annual plans: 2 months free

**Notable:** Raised $4.2M from Forerunner Ventures and Sequoia Capital (2022); integrates with Lalamove for fulfillment.

### 5. Atlas Kitchen
**Website:** https://www.atlas.kitchen

**Overview:** All-in-one restaurant software from Singapore, targeting independent and chain restaurants. Integrates POS, online ordering, QR scan-to-order, and aggregator sync with Grab, Deliveroo, Foodpanda.

**Key Features:**
- POS: payments, table management, role-based access, open items/discounts
- Atlas KDS (kitchen display)
- Atlas Online (online storefront)
- Atlas Scan to Order (QR table ordering)
- Atlas Aggregator (menu/order sync with delivery platforms)
- Atlas Connect (integration API)
- Inventory management, multi-location, reporting

**Target Customer:** Independent and chain restaurants; brands like Les Amis, SaladStop, The Coconut Club, Guzman y Gomez, Providore, Poke Theory

**Markets Served:** Singapore and Southeast Asia

**Pricing Model:** From $79/month; no hidden fees; no long-term contract

**Notable:** Integrates with Revel POS; offers financing options via Choco Up partnership for restaurants with cash flow needs.

### 6. Tablevibe
**Website:** https://www.tablevibe.co

**Overview:** All-in-one direct ordering platform enabling restaurants to accept delivery, pickup, and dine-in orders without third-party commission. Partners with GCash and delivery platforms to offer low-fee ordering.

**Key Features:**
- Unified platform for delivery, pickup, dine-in
- Customizable webshop for customer database
- Centralized delivery management with partner integration
- Social media integration (Instagram, Facebook, Google)
- Automated marketing (email, SMS)
- QR code feedback surveys and loyalty rewards
- GCash Food Hub and Scan-to-Order integration

**Target Customer:** Restaurants seeking to reduce delivery costs; initially focused on 5+ outlet chains; now serves 4,000+ restaurants including The Bistro Group, Kenny Rogers, Andok's, 24 Chicken, Chooks-To-Go

**Markets Served:** Philippines (primary); Southeast Asia, North America, Australia

**Pricing Model:** No setup fees; no fixed costs; 0% commission on orders; flat delivery fee from ~$6 (riders sourced)

**Notable:** Up to 72% cost reduction vs. 20–40% commission platforms; GCash partnership reaches 94M users; partners with DoorDash, Uber Direct.

## Additional Business Analysis Insights

### Funding & Traction
- **OlaClick:** $2M seed (2021); 40,000+ restaurants
- **Cococart:** $4.2M (Forerunner, Sequoia, 2022); 25,000+ merchants
- **Take.app:** 100,000+ registrations, 1M+ orders; strong organic growth

### Strategic Partnerships
- **Tablevibe + GCash:** Access to 94M users; Scan-to-Order in GCash app
- **Atlas + Choco Up:** Financing for restaurant growth
- **Cococart + Lalamove:** Fulfillment automation

### Market Gaps
- Most solutions target either full-service restaurants or general ecommerce; few are purpose-built for **quick service restaurants (QSR)** with high throughput
- WhatsApp-first players (Take.app, Cococart, OlaClick) often lack deep POS/KDS for in-store operations
- Full-stack players (Atlas, Avocado) tend to be higher-priced or US/LATAM-focused
- Tablevibe’s 0% commission model is compelling but initially targeted larger chains

### Unit Economics
- **Commission-based:** OlaClick (free tier), Take.app (free tier), Cococart (trial)
- **Subscription:** Take.app ($19–50), Cococart ($19–59+), Atlas ($79+), OlaClick ($8+)
- **Transaction/usage:** Avocado (transaction-based), Tablevibe (flat delivery fee)

## Opportunities for Smaller Quick Service Restaurants (QSR)

### 1. **Purpose-Built QSR Stack**
Most products are built for full-service or general ecommerce. QSRs need:
- **High-speed order entry** (handheld devices, self-service kiosks)
- **Minimal table management** (counter service, grab-and-go)
- **Fast kitchen workflows** (simple KDS, prep times, bump bars)
- **Offline resilience** for spotty connectivity

**Opportunity:** A lightweight POS + online ordering bundle optimized for counter-service, food trucks, and small QSRs.

### 2. **Affordable All-in-One**
- Atlas ($79/mo) and full POS suites are costly for single-location QSRs
- WhatsApp-first tools lack in-store POS
- Avocado’s no-fee model is US-focused

**Opportunity:** A single product combining POS, simple KDS, and online/WhatsApp ordering at a price point suitable for 1–3 location QSRs (e.g., $20–40/month).

### 3. **WhatsApp + POS Integration**
- Take.app and Cococart excel at WhatsApp but lack robust POS
- OlaClick has both but is LATAM-centric

**Opportunity:** WhatsApp order intake with automatic sync to a simple POS/KDS, so staff don’t re-key orders—especially valuable in markets where WhatsApp is the primary channel.

### 4. **Zero-Commission Direct Ordering for Small Restaurants**
- Tablevibe’s 0% model targets larger chains
- Small QSRs still rely on high-commission aggregators

**Opportunity:** A Tablevibe-like direct ordering model (0% commission, flat delivery) tailored to single-location and small-chain QSRs, with simple setup and low minimums.

### 5. **Emerging Market Localization**
- Payment methods: QRIS, GCash, PayMaya, PromptPay, UPI, Pix
- Language and currency support
- Local delivery/riders integration

**Opportunity:** A QSR-focused product with deep localization for 2–3 priority markets (e.g., Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam) including local payments and delivery.

### 6. **Simplified Operations**
- Inventory and reporting are often overbuilt for small QSRs
- Loyalty and marketing are secondary to speed and reliability

**Opportunity:** A “QSR essentials” product: order entry, payments, basic KDS, online/WhatsApp ordering, and minimal reporting—no complex inventory or CRM.

### 7. **Hardware Flexibility**
- Food trucks and pop-ups need portable, connectivity-flexible hardware
- Avocado addresses this but is US-only

**Opportunity:** Portable POS + printer setup with cellular backup, suitable for food trucks and outdoor QSRs in emerging markets.

---

## Recommended Product Direction for Small QSR Focus

A compelling product for smaller quick service restaurants could combine:

1. **Lightweight POS** – Counter-service optimized, minimal table management  
2. **Simple KDS** – Order display, bump bar, basic prep times  
3. **WhatsApp ordering** – Order intake and notifications without re-entry  
4. **Online storefront** – QR ordering, pickup/delivery time slots  
5. **0% commission direct ordering** – Flat delivery fee model  
6. **Affordable pricing** – $15–35/month or transaction-based for very small operators  
7. **Emerging market focus** – Philippines, Indonesia, or similar with local payments and delivery  

This would fill the gap between WhatsApp-only tools (Take.app, Cococart) and full restaurant suites (Atlas, OlaClick), while offering better economics than aggregators for small QSRs.
